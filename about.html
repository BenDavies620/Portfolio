Who I Am

Nice to meet you.

Hi, I'm Ben. I'm an aspiring web developer based in Norwich, Norfolk, currently developing my skills through hands-on projects and my training with Netmatters.

I've always enjoyed understanding how things work and solving problems along the way. Web development gives me a chance to combine that logical side with something much more creative. Taking an idea and turning it into something people can actually use.

I'm particularly interested in creating websites that feel clean, responsive and considered, where the small details matter just as much as the bigger picture.
